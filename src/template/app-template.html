How to play
The story plays out over four evenings, and you spend every one of them in your room.
Things you have
You have your phone, which is where the class group chat is, and it's where everything reaches you first.
Your phone also holds everything anyone has sent you, and a couple of apps for looking things up and comparing them, although those are slow and the evening keeps moving while you sit there.
And you have your bed, which ends the day whenever you decide you've done enough.
What you do with each evening is up to you.

Friendship to Nicole
How much Nicole trusts you.
The bar rises when you answer her, believe her, or stand up for her where other people can see it. It falls when you leave her on read, forward things about her, or stay quiet while the class talks.
The lower it gets, the slower and shorter her replies get. If it empties, she stops answering you — and that doesn't come back.
Checking things never moves either bar.
Popularity in 10b
What the class thinks of you.
The bar rises when you go along with everyone, and it rises most when you say something and can back it up. It falls when you contradict the group with nothing to show — after that, people stop taking your word for anything.
The lower it gets, the more they push back at you and leave you out. The higher it gets, the more they assume you're on their side.
Checking things never moves either bar.
