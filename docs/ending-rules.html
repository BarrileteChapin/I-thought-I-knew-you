Decision flow

Ending cards

1

Best public outcome

checks ≥ 4 ∧ public support ∧ Nicole ≥ 50 ∧ group ≥ 35

“You waited for the truth. Then you spoke it aloud. She heard you twice.”

You checked first, then stood up where people could hear — and kept both relationships.

2

Right, but costly

checks ≥ 4 ∧ public support ∧ Nicole ≥ 50 ∧ group < 35

“You told the truth to the room. The room grew quieter around you.”

You stood up for her correctly, but the class pulled away.

3

Private loyalty

checks ≥ 2 ∧ not public ∧ DM ≥ 3 ∧ Nicole ≥ 50

“You were the only one who knew. And you kept it between the two of you.”

You knew enough to help, but only told Nicole — never the group.

4

Quiet contact

checks ≥ 2 ∧ not public ∧ DM ≥ 1

“A few words in the dark. None in the light.”

Some private messages; nothing said where the class could see.

5

Looked, unused

checks ≥ 2 (and none of 1–4)

“You found the crack in their story. You let them keep telling it.”

You noticed the rumor was wrong, but you didn’t turn that into care.

6

Little investigation

else · checks < 2

“I thought I knew you, {name}.”

You barely checked. The week mostly ran without you resisting it.

What “publicly supported” means

  • nicoleHigh: sam ≥ 50 and not silence-locked
  • groupOk: group ≥ 35
  • dmCount: max(dmAnswered, stats.chat.dm)

Side effects

  • DM send → dmAnswered++
  • Group supportive / pile_on → postsWith / postsWithout (by that day’s certainty)
  • Group supportive → final.post = 'support'
  • Ledger shows “In the chats” tone counts
  • To keep both bars high: investigate first → speak with proof → keep answering Nicole
