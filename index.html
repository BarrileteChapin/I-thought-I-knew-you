Media literacy matters where the rumour actually lands.

For a teenager it isn't a news story to fact-check later. It's a video in the class chat, sent by someone they trust, about someone else they trust — and the room is already reacting. Generative AI made that easier: a cut clip, a recycled photo, a cloned voice. The missing piece isn't a lecture. It's the habit of pausing first.

SIFT. Four moves before you react.

When a rumour hits the chat, these four habits buy you time to check it — based on Mike Caulfield's SIFT method (2019). The game is practice: pause, look closer, then decide.

  1. S

    Stop

    Don’t react or forward yet. Take a breath.

  2. I

    Investigate the source

    Who posted this, and what do you know about them?

  3. F

    Find better coverage

    Has anyone else reported the same claim?

  4. T

    Trace the original

    Find the first version — where and when it appeared.
